01 / SCOPE

先定義預測目標與決策邊界Define the prediction target and decision boundary

設計社群動態中的廣告點擊預測。第一版負責為合格候選輸出可信的 pCTR；候選檢索、競價、pacing 與使用者體驗限制是相鄰元件，必須說明契約。

Design click prediction for ads in a social feed. The first version outputs trustworthy pCTR for eligible candidates. Retrieval, auction, pacing, and user-experience constraints are adjacent components with explicit contracts.

貫穿全篇的假設Running assumptions

使用者打開 feed，系統需為一組合格廣告預測「實際曝光後 W 內的有效落地頁點擊」。範例假設每秒 1,000 個請求、每次精排 200 個廣告、精排服務 p99 預算 80 ms。所有流量、窗口、模型尺寸與 SLO 都是設計練習的假設，應向面試官確認；不是 Meta 公開規格。

Illustrative assumptions: 1,000 requests/s, 200 candidates per ranking request, and an 80 ms p99 ranking-service budget. For CPC ads, bid × pCTR can contribute to expected bid value, but is not a complete auction formula. Delivery also depends on quality, budgets, pacing, and eligibility. Keep the prediction service's output interpretable as a probability and let downstream logic define utility. Arbitrary business-weighted labels do not automatically preserve pCTR semantics.

為什麼只有 event_time < impression_time 還不夠？Why is event_time < impression_time insufficient?

某個行為雖發生得早，卻在曝光後才被系統收到。若 offline aggregate 使用它，而 online 當時不可能知道，就有 availability leakage。理想用 serving snapshot；若重建特徵，同時檢查 event time 和資料可用時間，並以 replay 比對線上輸入。

An event may occur earlier but arrive after the impression. Including it offline leaks information unavailable to serving. Prefer logged serving snapshots; when reconstructing features, check both event time and availability time, and compare replayed tensors with online inputs.

要不要完全按 user 切分？Should users be completely disjoint across splits?

主要評估要模擬未來服務，故以時間切分，同一使用者可以再次出現；這本身不是洩漏。另用 disjoint users／ads 評估冷啟動，並說明它回答的是不同問題。所有標籤衍生統計仍須嚴格 point-in-time。

Use temporal splits to represent future serving; recurring users are not inherently leakage. Add disjoint-user/ad evaluations for cold start and explain that they answer a different question. Label-derived statistics must remain strictly point-in-time.

04 / SAMPLING

抽樣、曝光偏差與機率校準Sampling, exposure bias, and probability calibration

類別不平衡、計算成本與曝光偏差是三個問題。negative sampling 可以省算力，但不會自動修好選擇偏差。

Class imbalance, compute cost, and exposure bias are distinct problems. Negative sampling saves compute; it does not automatically fix selection bias.

q = P(y=1 | x, retained)
s1 = P(retained | y=1),  s0 = P(retained | y=0)
q/(1-q) = (s1/s0) * p/(1-p)
p = s0*q / [s1*(1-q) + s0*q]
logit(p) = logit(q) + log(s0/s1)
Example: q=0.10, s1=1, s0=0.10 → p≈0.010989

抽樣如何改變 pCTR？How does sampling change pCTR?

修正後的機率 pCorrected probability p: 1.0989%

q 必須介於 0 與 1；保留率必須大於 0 且不超過 1。q must be between 0 and 1; retention rates must be greater than 0 and at most 1.

互動計算只示範 Bayes 修正；假設保留率只依 label、q 在抽樣分布已校準，且未另外用 inverse weighting 修正。

This calculator demonstrates Bayes correction only: retention depends on the label, q is calibrated on the sampled distribution, and inverse weighting has not already corrected the model.

這是 Bayes rule 的推導，前提是保留率只依 label（或已知每個 x 的保留率），而 q 確實代表抽樣分布。另一條路是使用 1/s_y 的 inverse-probability weighted BCE 估原始風險；兩條路不能不加分析地疊用，否則重複修正。Hard-negative mining 依 x 選樣，不能套一個全域負例率就宣稱已還原。

This follows from Bayes' rule when retention depends only on the label, or when retention conditional on x is known, and q represents the sampled distribution. Alternatively, inverse-probability weighted BCE using 1/s_y estimates original risk. Do not blindly combine weighting with posterior correction: that can double-correct. Hard-negative mining depends on x and cannot generally be undone with one global negative-retention rate.

為什麼不直接用 SMOTE 或 focal loss？Why not immediately use SMOTE or focal loss?

任意插值類別 ID 或 embeddings 不一定代表有效曝光。Focal loss 重新加權難例，可能改善某些目標，但不能假設輸出仍是原始 CTR。先以正確標籤、BCE、抽樣修正建立 baseline；任何新 loss 都需比較校準、排序、slice 與成本。

Interpolating categorical IDs or embeddings need not create valid impressions. Focal loss reweights hard examples and may help some objectives, but its output is not automatically the original CTR. Establish correct labels, BCE, and sampling correction first; evaluate any alternative loss on calibration, discrimination, slices, and cost.

A high CTR from a tiny sample is not reliable quality; permanently suppressing unknown ads also prevents learning.

45 分鐘回答流程與深入追問A 45-minute answer flow and deeper follow-ups

沿六步回答，每一步都說出一個具體選擇、一個取捨和一個驗證方法。先閉卷口說，再展開中英答案。

Use six stages, each with a concrete choice, tradeoff, and validation method. Rehearse without the text before opening either language.

00–05 · 釐清產品目標00–05 · Clarify the product goal

我先把任務限於社群 feed 的精排 CTR，預測有效曝光後 W 內的落地頁點擊。確認版位、候選量、延遲、click 定義和可用特徵。模型輸出條件機率，下游再結合競價與品質限制；線上主要價值指標和負回饋、延遲護欄需先對齊。

I will scope this to CTR prediction for social-feed ranking: a landing-page click within W after a valid impression. I will clarify placement, candidate volume, latency, click semantics, and available features. The model produces a conditional probability; downstream auction and quality rules consume it. We should agree on a primary value metric and negative-feedback/latency guardrails first.

05–13 · 資料與標籤05–13 · Data and labels

一筆樣本是一筆 rendered impression，以 impression ID join 去重後的 click。使用 event time 定義窗口，arrival time 控制資料可用性；成熟且來源健康後才定案負例。特徵用 serving snapshot 或雙時間條件重建，按時間切 train／validation／calibration／test。若抽負例，保存保留率並修正 posterior 或 loss。未曝光候選不直接標 0。

One example is a rendered impression joined to deduplicated clicks by impression ID. Event time defines the target window; arrival time governs availability. Finalize negatives only after maturity with healthy sources. Use serving snapshots or time-aware reconstruction, split chronologically into train/validation/calibration/test, and log retention rates if sampling negatives. Correct either the posterior or loss appropriately. Unshown candidates are not zero-label examples.

13–25 · Baseline／模型13–25 · Baselines and models

先用 LR／FTRL 和 user、ad、context、平滑歷史統計建立 baseline。固定資料比較 DeepFM 或 DCNv2 的交互收益；再以行為歷史是否有 candidate-specific 訊號決定加 DIN 或序列模型。解釋 BCE、sampling、OOV 和模型輸出。用 pooled／shuffled／ordered history、content ablation 與公平調參驗證假說，不因模型新就選它。

Start with LR/FTRL and user, ad, context, and smoothed history features. With fixed data, test interaction gains from DeepFM or DCNv2. Add DIN or sequence models only for a hypothesis about candidate-specific history or temporal signal. Explain BCE, sampling, OOV handling, and output semantics. Use pooled/shuffled/ordered histories, content ablations, and fair tuning budgets to test the hypothesis rather than selecting by novelty.

25–33 · 訓練與部署25–33 · Training and deployment

先批次重訓並獨立量 feature／model age，視收益再加近線更新。高 cardinality embeddings 估 optimizer memory、sharding 和 communication。Serving 批次處理候選、共用 user-only 計算，設定 feature deadline 與已測 fallback。把 vocabulary、encoder、模型與 calibrator 作相容 bundle 發布，經 parity、shadow、canary 後放量，資料污染時凍結更新。

Begin with batch training and measure feature/model age separately before adding nearline updates. Budget optimizer memory, sharding, and communication for high-cardinality embeddings. Batch candidates, share user-only computation, and define feature deadlines and evaluated fallbacks. Release vocabulary, encoders, model, and calibrator as a compatible bundle through parity checks, shadowing, and canaries; freeze updates when data is contaminated.

33–41 · 離線評估／線上實驗33–41 · Offline and online evaluation

離線以未來資料報 log loss、明確定義的 NE、AUC 和 calibration；按新廣告、版位、活躍度與 freshness 切片，使用適合相關結構的 uncertainty。線上先確認分流與 SRM，預先定義主要指標、MDE、觀察窗和 guardrails。共享 advertiser budget 可能造成干擾，需要評估隔離或不同 randomization。不能由 AUC 推導收入 lift。

On future data, report log loss, explicitly defined NE, AUC, and calibration, sliced by new ads, placement, activity, and freshness with dependence-aware uncertainty. Online, validate assignment and SRM, and prespecify the primary metric, MDE, observation window, and guardrails. Shared advertiser budgets may require isolation or a different randomization design. AUC does not establish revenue lift.

41–45 · 失敗案例與取捨41–45 · Failures and tradeoffs

若 click source 中斷，我會先凍結錯誤 labels 和更新，保留健康 bundle，再回補；若 user embedding 過期或版本不符，走相容 fallback。最後以資料新鮮度、模型容量與成本的實驗曲線決定下一步。我會明確區分已知證據、設計假設和仍需驗證的部分。

If the click source fails, freeze bad labels and updates, preserve the healthy bundle, and backfill. For stale or incompatible user embeddings, use a compatible fallback. Choose the next investment from measured freshness, capacity, and cost curves. Distinguish established evidence, design assumptions, and remaining validation.

追問：只有 click logs，沒有曝光紀錄？Follow-up: clicks but no impression logs?

There is no denominator or record of click opportunities, so standard impression CTR is not identifiable from those logs alone. Improve logging or redefine an identifiable task such as click-conditioned next-item prediction, explicitly distinguishing it from pCTR. Random unclicked ads do not reconstruct the missing exposure mechanism.

追問：校準比例等於 1，還有問題嗎？Follow-up: calibration ratio is 1; are we done?

沒有。高估一群、低估另一群可能相互抵消；不同分數區間也可能失準。檢查 reliability plot、slice counts、Brier／loss 與 ranker 使用機率的區域。校準器的資料分布和時間也要匹配目標流量。

No. Overprediction in one group can offset underprediction in another, and score ranges can be miscalibrated. Inspect reliability plots, slice counts, Brier/loss, and the probability ranges used by the ranker. The calibrator's data distribution and time period must match target traffic.

追問：你如何設計一個有研究價值的下一步？Follow-up: what is a research-worthy next step?

Propose an identifiable hypothesis, such as whether diverse event types outperform equally long click-only histories. Control event count, time span, capacity, and tuning budget; ablate action type, order, and content independently. Evaluate multiple periods, cold-start slices, uncertainty, and cost-quality curves, retaining negative results and failure analysis.
