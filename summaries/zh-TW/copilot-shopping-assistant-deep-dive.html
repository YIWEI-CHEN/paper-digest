01 / SCOPE

把購物成功定義成可驗證的結果Define shopping success as a verifiable outcome

設計一個 Copilot 購物助理：理解需求、檢索商品與廣告、查詢即時價格與庫存，產生有依據且清楚標示贊助內容的推薦。第一版聚焦探索、比較與推薦；交易執行是另行設計的延伸。

Design a Copilot shopping assistant that understands needs, retrieves products and ads, verifies current prices and availability, and produces grounded recommendations with clear sponsorship labels. Scope the first version to discovery, comparison, and recommendation; transaction execution is a separate extension.

貫穿全篇的案例Running example

「找 150 美元以內、適合雨天通勤、放得下我的 15 吋筆電，週五前能送到的背包。比較三款，廣告請標示。」地區、筆電實際尺寸、稅運是否包含在預算內，尚未確認。以下商品、價格、容量與時間都是教學假設。

“Find a backpack under $150 for rainy commutes that fits my 15-inch laptop and arrives by Friday. Compare three options and label ads.” Delivery region, actual laptop dimensions, and whether the budget includes tax and shipping are still unknown. Products, prices, scale, and timing below are illustrative assumptions.

Ask clarifying questions when they change the decision. Delivery region changes the eligible set; unrelated details can wait. Resolve an ambiguous Friday to an explicit date and confirm when needed. Track confirmed, inferred, and unknown values rather than silently promoting an inference into a user requirement.

為什麼不直接讓 LLM 推薦三個品牌？Why not just ask an LLM for three brands?

我們要選的是現在可購買的特定 variant／offer。品牌知識無法保證預算、尺寸、配送與庫存，也不能產生可稽核的贊助來源。LLM 負責語言理解與證據說明，商品選擇與動態事實要有明確資料路徑。

We need purchasable variants and offers, not brand names. Model knowledge cannot establish current price, fit, delivery, stock, or auditable sponsorship. Use the LLM for language understanding and evidence-based explanation, with explicit data paths for selection and dynamic facts.

02 / ARCHITECTURE

兩次檢索、一道驗證邊界Two retrieval tasks, one verification boundary

商品檢索回答「選哪些商品」；證據檢索回答「哪些資料支持比較與推薦」。即時商務工具確認 offer；渲染器依可信欄位呈現價格與 Sponsored 標示。下圖是重新設計的教學架構，參考公開概念，沒有複製原圖。

Product retrieval decides which items to select; evidence retrieval finds support for comparisons and recommendations. Live commerce tools verify offers, and a renderer displays prices and sponsorship from trusted fields. The diagram is an original teaching design informed by public concepts, not a reproduction of a source diagram.

01 · 有界的工作流程01 · A bounded workflow

先用固定的 parse → retrieve → verify → rank → explain 流程；模型只在條件不明或候選不足時選擇澄清／改寫。設工具次數、時間、token 上限，保留有限次補取候選。

Start with parse → retrieve → verify → rank → explain. Let the model choose clarification or reformulation when constraints are unclear or candidates are insufficient. Bound tool calls, elapsed time, tokens, and candidate-refill attempts.

02 · 商品身分一路保留02 · Preserve identity end to end

用 product_id、variant_id、offer_id 連接檢索、商務工具與證據。只用名稱連接會把不同尺寸、賣家與版本混在一起。

Carry product_id, variant_id, and offer_id through retrieval, commerce tools, and evidence. Joining by name alone can mix sizes, sellers, and revisions.

03 · 回答也是資料結構03 · An answer is also a data structure

生成器輸出候選 ID、比較 claim 與 evidence_id；後端解析工具回傳的價格／庫存與贊助欄位，前端再組合卡片。不能只用 prompt 要求模型「記得正確」。

The generator returns candidate IDs, comparison claims, and evidence IDs. Backend code resolves tool-backed commerce fields and sponsorship before rendering cards. A prompt asking the model to be correct is insufficient.

可先用索引價格和庫存旗標降低搜尋成本，但它們是粗篩訊號。最終候選需要權威查詢；若大量候選失效，按預算補取下一批。候選補取是 recall／工具成本的取捨，而不是無限 agent loop。

Indexed prices and stock flags can reduce search cost, but they are coarse filters. Verify final candidates against authoritative services and refill within a budget when many fail. Refill trades recall against tool cost; it should not become an unbounded agent loop.

03 / DATA

資料契約、標籤與時間語意Data contracts, labels, and time semantics

一個 dataset 無法同時代表商品相關性、個人偏好、證據完整性與交易正確性。先定義每一層的樣本與標籤，再決定如何訓練及評估。

One dataset cannot represent product relevance, individual preference, evidence sufficiency, and transaction correctness at once. Define examples and labels separately for each layer before training or evaluation.

Build point-in-time features using event time and monitor arrival lag with ingestion time. Deduplicate exposures and purchases. Distinguish out-of-window purchases from in-window events that arrive late. Do not force immature outcomes to zero; keep separate label definitions for ad clicks, organic clicks, and purchases.

公開資料的正確用途Use public datasets within their limits

ESCI 用於 query–product relevance，保留官方 query split，再從 training queries 切 validation；未標註商品不等於 irrelevant，Recall@K 是相對已知 judgments。Amazon-M2 用於 session next-item prediction；不是購買或庫存標籤。Reviews 2023 的 review time 不是 purchase time，metadata 也未必是歷史當下快照。不同資料集先獨立做實驗，沒有驗證 ID 對應就不強行 join。

Use ESCI for query–product relevance, preserving its query split and deriving validation from training queries. Unjudged items are not automatically irrelevant; Recall@K is relative to known judgments. Amazon-M2 supports session next-item prediction, not purchase or stock labels. A Reviews 2023 review timestamp is not purchase time, and metadata may not be a historical snapshot. Keep experiments separate unless cross-dataset identity mapping is verified.

如何避免切分造成過度樂觀？How do you avoid optimistic evaluation splits?

生產日誌依時間切分，session 不跨組，所有聚合特徵按當時可用資訊計算。ESCI 沒有可用的線上時間序列就不要宣稱 temporal validation；用官方 split／query grouping。另建新商品、罕見規格與中英混合查詢切片，資料來源不支持的能力要用獨立測試。

For production logs, split by time, keep sessions intact, and compute features as of the decision. Do not claim temporal validation for ESCI without the required timestamps; use its official split and query grouping. Add cold-start, rare-attribute, and mixed-language slices, with separate tests for capabilities the dataset does not cover.

04 / FEATURES

從需求到可學習的特徵Translate needs into learnable features

區分用於精確條件判斷的欄位、用於模型排序的訊號，以及用於解釋的證據。三者可能來自同一資料，但不能互相取代。

Distinguish fields used for exact constraints, signals used for ranking, and evidence used for explanation. They may originate from the same data, but are not interchangeable.

05 / MODELS

Baseline、loss 與模型升級的證據Baselines, losses, and evidence for model upgrades

先建立 BM25＋結構化 filters＋簡單排序＋工具驗證的 baseline。每次只針對已觀察到的錯誤加入複雜度；不能把大模型名稱當成設計理由。

Start with BM25, structured filters, simple ranking, and tool verification. Add complexity to address an observed failure mode; a larger model name is not a design rationale.

Lretrieval = −log [ exp(sim(q, p+)/τ) / Σp∈C exp(sim(q, p)/τ) ]
RRF(p) = Σr 1 / (k + rankr(p))

C 是有定義的訓練候選池，τ 是溫度；in-batch negatives 可能含正確替代品，應 mask 已知 positives，並審查 hard negatives。從訓練 split 的 BM25／舊模型採樣相似但不合規商品，不從 test labels 挖負例。RRF 的 k 與 τ 在 validation 選擇，不把教學預設當最優值。

C is a defined training candidate pool and τ is temperature. In-batch negatives can include valid substitutes: mask known positives and audit hard negatives. Mine similar but incompatible products from training-split BM25 or a previous model, not test labels. Tune RRF k and τ on validation rather than treating defaults as optimal.

ESCI 不是通用的固定效用排序ESCI is not a universal utility ordering

『找背包』時配件通常不如替代背包；『我已買背包，還需要什麼』時 complement 可能最有價值。先定義意圖，再定義 label→gain。若 pPurchase 是曝光後購買機率，不要再乘一次 pCTR；只有條件定義相容時才能相乘。

For 'find a backpack', an accessory is usually less useful than a substitute bag; for 'what else do I need with this bag', a complement may be best. Define intent before label-to-gain mapping. Do not multiply an impression-conditional purchase probability by pCTR again; multiplication requires compatible conditional definitions.

為什麼不讓 LLM 對所有商品直接排序？Why not let an LLM rank every product?

成本與 context 限制使全量評分不實際，也不利於穩定校準和重播。先用低成本 retrieval 縮小集合，再在受控候選上比較 cross-encoder 與 LLM reranker 的增益、位置敏感度、延遲和成本；LLM 只能重排有效 ID。

Scoring the full catalog is expensive and context-limited, and complicates calibration and replay. Retrieve cheaply first, then compare cross-encoders and LLM rerankers on controlled candidates for gain, order sensitivity, latency, and cost. An LLM may reorder only valid IDs.

06 / EVIDENCE

RAG 的單位是有範圍的證據RAG operates on scoped evidence

有引用不代表引用支持主張；支持主張也不代表資料仍然有效。把 evidence selection、claim generation、source freshness 與完整性分開測量。

A citation may not support its claim, and supporting evidence may no longer be current. Measure evidence selection, claim generation, source freshness, and completeness separately.

Chunk 與 metadataChunks and metadata

以 product／variant 和語意章節切分；欄位標題、單位、來源時間一起保存。短商品卡可視為一筆記錄，長說明書才分 chunk；chunk 大小是實驗變數。

Chunk by product/variant and semantic section, preserving headings, units, and source dates. A short product card can remain one record; long manuals need sections. Treat chunk size as an experimental variable.

Hybrid 與 rerankingHybrid retrieval and reranking

先依有效商品／variant／locale 限定證據範圍，再合併 lexical 和 dense 結果；跨商品政策另外處理。Contextual prefixes 幫找回背景，但原始來源才是主張依據。

Scope evidence to eligible products, variants, and locales before fusing lexical and dense results; handle shared policies separately. Contextual prefixes aid retrieval, but original source text remains the evidence for claims.

Claim–evidence mappingClaim–evidence mapping

每個可驗證比較欄位都要映射 evidence IDs：容量、尺寸、防水等級、限制。沒有足夠支持時顯示未知或縮小措辭；評論只能以評論觀察的方式呈現。

Map each verifiable comparison field to evidence IDs: capacity, dimensions, waterproof rating, limitations. Mark unsupported claims unknown or narrow the wording; present review observations as review observations.

故障與重試Failures and retries

14 / INTERVIEW

45 分鐘回答流程與深入追問A 45-minute answer flow and deeper follow-ups

練習時先口說再展開答案。每一步都包含具體選擇、理由與可推翻該選擇的實驗；不要只唸服務清單。

Rehearse aloud before opening the answers. At each stage, state a choice, its rationale, and an experiment that could overturn it; do not merely list services.

00–05 · 釐清目標：先說什麼？00–05 · Clarify the goal: how do you open?

我先把成功定義成找到符合需求、當下可買且有可信比較依據的商品。確認場景、catalog 範圍、地區、延遲、是否包含下單，再分硬限制與偏好。以背包案例確認尺寸、預算範圍和到貨日；若資訊不足，只追問影響候選的項目。

I define success as finding suitable, currently purchasable products with trustworthy comparison evidence. I clarify the surface, catalog, region, latency, and whether checkout is included, then separate constraints from preferences. For the backpack example, I confirm fit, budget scope, and delivery date, asking only questions that materially change the candidate set.

05–13 · 資料與標籤：如何落地？05–13 · Data and labels: what is concrete?

我將 catalog、動態 offers、文件證據與曝光 outcome 分開，使用 product／variant／offer IDs 連接。搜尋用 relevance judgments，個人化用 session／exposure outcomes，回答用 claim support 和工具快照。所有時序特徵只用當下已知資料，成熟窗口和未觀察樣本各自定義。

I separate catalog records, dynamic offers, document evidence, and exposure outcomes, joined by product, variant, and offer IDs. Search uses relevance judgments; personalization uses sessions and exposure outcomes; answers use claim support and tool snapshots. Temporal features use information available at decision time, with explicit maturity windows and unobserved-sample handling.

13–25 · Baseline／模型：如何選？13–25 · Baselines and models: how do you choose?

先用 BM25、filters 與簡單排序驗證資料，再加 dense retrieval 處理用途語意、cross-encoder 處理候選交互。解釋 loss、hard negatives 和 ESCI 意圖差異。證據檢索只看有效商品範圍，LLM 產生有引用的比較；模型升級必須在同等成本／延遲下通過 ablation。

I validate the pipeline with BM25, filters, and simple ranking, then add dense retrieval for use-case semantics and a cross-encoder for richer interactions. I explain losses, hard negatives, and intent-dependent ESCI gains. Evidence retrieval is scoped to valid products; the LLM explains with citations. Upgrades must survive ablations under comparable cost and latency.

25–33 · 訓練與部署：如何保證線上可用？25–33 · Training and serving: how does it operate?

我用批次工具確認 offer 的價格、庫存、地區與配送，未知就保留未知。商品卡和贊助標籤由可信欄位渲染。模型、索引、特徵、prompt 與工具 schema 共同版本化；用 shadow／canary、負載量測與完整 bundle rollback。快取可以重用，但動態事實要重新驗證。

I batch-verify offer price, stock, region, and delivery, preserving unknown states. Trusted fields render product cards and sponsorship. Models, indexes, features, prompts, and tool schemas are versioned together, with shadow/canary validation, load measurement, and bundle rollback. Cached work can be reused, but dynamic facts must be revalidated.

33–41 · 評估：怎麼證明有進步？33–41 · Evaluation: how do you show improvement?

元件用 recall、NDCG、constraint accuracy、claim support 和工具正確性；端到端用固定環境的 task pass、多次 trial、成本與 useful latency。線上穩定分流，先看 SRM／日誌品質，等購買與退貨成熟再判斷。對共享廣告預算考慮組間干擾。

I use recall, NDCG, constraint accuracy, claim support, and tool correctness for components. End-to-end evaluation uses task pass in fixed environments, repeated trials, cost, and useful latency. Online, randomize consistently, inspect SRM/logging first, and let purchases and returns mature. Account for interference from shared ad budgets.

41–45 · 失敗案例與取捨：如何收尾？41–45 · Failures and tradeoffs: how do you close?

如果工具價格超過預算，我會排除並補取候選，不修改使用者限制；若證據缺乏防水規格，就標未知而不是推測；超預算的高出價廣告同樣被擋下。我會以任務成功、資料新鮮度、尾延遲和每成功任務成本決定下一個投資點。

If a verified price exceeds budget, I exclude it and refill rather than changing the user's constraint. If waterproofing evidence is missing, I mark it unknown. A high-bid ad that exceeds budget is also excluded. Task success, freshness, tail latency, and cost per successful task determine the next investment.

追問：沒有符合全部需求的商品怎麼辦？Follow-up: what if no product meets every requirement?

區分真的無解、檢索不足、工具未知。先在預算內增加召回或驗證；仍無解則說明哪個限制卡住並請使用者決定是否放寬。不要自動提高預算、把 estimated arrival 當保證，或用贊助結果填滿三個名額。

Distinguish no feasible match from retrieval failure or unknown tool state. Expand retrieval/verification within budget, then explain the blocking constraint and let the user choose whether to relax it. Do not silently raise the budget, turn an arrival estimate into a guarantee, or fill three slots with sponsored mismatches.

追問：offline 指標變好，線上滿意度卻下降？Follow-up: offline gains but lower online satisfaction?

依序查 candidate 分布、constraint violations、offer freshness、證據支持與 latency。也可能是 NDCG gain 設計不合任務、個人化蓋過當下需求、或廣告負載增加。用固定候選／工具 fixtures 分層 replay，再以元件 A/B 做因果定位。

Inspect candidate distribution, constraint violations, offer freshness, evidence support, and latency. NDCG gains may be misaligned with intent, personalization may override current needs, or ad load may have increased. Replay layers with fixed candidates/tool fixtures and isolate causes through component experiments.
