01 / SCOPE

產品目標與問題定義Product goals and problem definition

先定義使用者正在完成的購物任務，再決定要預測哪一種行為。首頁、商品頁、購物車與補貨推薦不應共用一個未加區分的目標。

Define the shopping task before choosing the prediction target. Homepage discovery, product pages, carts, and replenishment should not share an undifferentiated objective.

本篇的設計範圍Scope of this design

以電商首頁的 20 個個人化商品為主，支援登入與匿名 session；商品頁的替代品與互補品作為延伸。假設 1,000 萬商品、尖峰 5,000 QPS、推薦 API p95 150 ms。這些都是用來推導容量與取捨的面試假設，不是 Amazon 公開的流量、SLA 或現行內部架構。

Focus on 20 personalized homepage products for signed-in users and anonymous sessions, with substitutes and complements on product pages as extensions. Assume 10 million items, 5,000 peak QPS, and a 150 ms recommendation API p95. These are interview sizing assumptions, not disclosed Amazon traffic, SLAs, or its current internal architecture.

A primary metric could be matured valid orders per assigned user, with explicit cancellation and return windows. CTR diagnoses the funnel. Guardrails include site-wide conversion, repeat visits, satisfaction, cancellations, returns, latency, and unavailable recommendations. For discovery, assess downstream quality of new-item and new-category exploration rather than clicks alone.

I would clarify the surface, discovery versus purchase completion, anonymous traffic, and the distinction between products, variants, and offers. Then I would agree on the primary metric, latency, and inventory freshness. I will start with personalized homepage recommendations that improve valid purchases while protecting experience. If ads are included, sponsorship provenance and visible labeling must survive ranking.

02 / ARCHITECTURE

多階段架構與責任邊界Multistage architecture and responsibilities

先便宜地找到值得看的一小群商品，再把昂貴特徵與模型用在這一群。候選數與時間預算都必須由實測校準。

Find a small promising set cheaply, then spend richer features and model capacity on that set. Candidate counts and timing budgets require measurement.

每個 retriever 先產生比配額略多的候選，合併後依 stable item ID 去重，保留所有來源。各來源原始分數通常不可直接比較；交給校準／ranker 使用來源、來源名次與分數。早期過濾減少浪費，最後再查驗會快速變動的庫存與可配送性。

Each retriever overfetches within a budget. Merge by stable item ID while retaining all source memberships. Raw scores from different retrievers are generally not comparable; expose calibrated scores, source indicators, and source ranks to the ranker. Early filtering reduces waste; a final check handles rapidly changing inventory and deliverability.

為什麼不直接對 1,000 萬商品跑最好的 ranker？Why not run the best ranker on all 10 million items?

精排通常有使用者與候選的交叉、序列 attention 和大量特徵查詢，成本隨候選數增加。先以可預計算的 item 表示召回，再用 query-dependent 交互精排，能讓延遲與成本可控。代價是 retrieval 形成上限；如果 relevant item 根本沒進候選，ranker 無法補救，所以要分開診斷召回損失與排序損失。

A rich ranker often uses user–candidate crosses, sequence attention, and many feature lookups, so cost grows with candidate count. Retrieval with precomputed item representations followed by query-dependent ranking bounds latency and cost. Retrieval becomes a ceiling: the ranker cannot recover an item that never entered the pool, so I would diagnose retrieval and ranking losses separately.

04 / LABELS

標籤、負例與偏差Labels, negatives, and bias

先定義觀察機制與分母，才有資格談 loss。未曝光的商品、曝光未點擊、點擊未購買，是三種不同的訊號。

Define the observation process and denominator before the loss. Unexposed items, exposed-but-unclicked items, and clicked-but-unpurchased items are different signals.

最容易解釋的起步方案是等完整觀察窗成熟，再加入該任務訓練。若要吸收新鮮資料，可使用延遲回饋模型或明確的部分標籤策略，但不能把還沒買誤當永遠不買。Last-touch attribution 是記帳規則，不能證明推薦造成了額外購買；增量效果仍需可信的實驗設計。

The simplest defensible starting point is to train a task only after its observation window matures. Fresher data can use delayed-feedback models or explicit partial-label policies, but not-yet-purchased must not mean never-purchased. Last-touch attribution is an accounting rule, not proof that a recommendation caused an incremental purchase; incrementality needs credible experimentation.

三種偏差不能用同一個 correction 解決Three biases need different treatments

負例抽樣偏差：訓練看到的商品頻率受 sampler 改變。曝光／位置偏差：舊策略決定誰有機會被看見。延遲／選擇偏差：成熟 cohort 或只看點擊後購買會改變樣本分布。Log-Q 只針對特定抽樣機制；IPS 需要已知或可合理估計的 propensity、support 與方差控制；多任務 learning 也不會自動解決全部問題。

Negative-sampling bias comes from the sampler changing item frequency. Exposure/position bias comes from the old policy determining what can be seen. Delay/selection bias comes from maturity cohorts or observing conversion only after clicks. Log-Q addresses a specific sampling mechanism. IPS needs known or defensibly estimated propensities, support, and variance control. Multitask learning does not fix all three automatically.

如果為了訓練速度只保留 10% 負例？What if you retain only 10% of negatives to train faster?

保留抽樣機率與樣本權重，在符合抽樣假設下修正 loss 或 posterior，並以未改變母體比例的驗證集做機率校準。只看抽樣資料上的 AUC 或 log loss 不能保證線上機率正確。抽樣若依使用者、品類或難度而變，不能只用一個全域常數修正。

Retain sampling probabilities and weights, then correct the loss or posterior under the sampling assumptions. Calibrate on validation data that preserves the population distribution. AUC or log loss on sampled data alone does not guarantee correct serving probabilities. If sampling varies by user, category, or difficulty, a single global correction is insufficient.

For cold-start items, derive representations from text, images, and structured attributes. ID dropout or gating can reduce reliance on memorized popular IDs. LLM 或多模態模型應放在哪裡？Where would an LLM or multimodal model help?

先用在離線商品屬性擷取、文字標準化或內容 embeddings，特別評估新品與長尾的增益。保留來源與驗證規則，避免把生成的錯誤規格當事實。只有當線上收益超過延遲與成本，才加入請求路徑；單純商品推薦並不需要先生成一段文字。

Start with offline attribute extraction, text normalization, or content embeddings, and measure gains on new and tail items. Preserve provenance and validation rules so generated specifications do not become unverified facts. Add models to the request path only when value justifies latency and cost; product recommendation does not inherently require text generation.

06 / BASELINE

強 baseline：熱門與 item-to-itemStrong baselines: popularity and item-to-item

先做可解釋、可服務且有合理調參的 baseline，讓複雜模型必須證明額外價值。Item-to-item 是重要起點，不等於只能提供靜態推薦。

Begin with interpretable, servable, reasonably tuned baselines so complex models must demonstrate added value. Item-to-item is a useful starting point, not a requirement for static recommendations.

B0：分群熱門B0: segmented popularity

依 locale、品類與時間窗計算，先過濾供給；記錄這個 baseline 的 cold-start coverage、有效候選數與延遲。熱門不是隨便取全站銷量前 20。

Co-view 就是替代品，co-buy 就是互補品嗎？Does co-view mean substitutes and co-buy mean complements?

Be explicit about output semantics: similarity, click probability, conditional conversion rate, and expected utility are different quantities. Choose the target before increasing model complexity.

pCTR × pCVR 何時成立？When is pCTR × pCVR valid?

只有當 purchase 事件明確被定義為 click 之後的轉換，且 pCVR = P(purchase | click, exposure, x)，才可用 P(click | exposure, x) × pCVR 表示該路徑的購買率。若 head 已直接預測 P(purchase | exposure, x)，再乘 pCTR 會重複折扣；有 view-through purchase 時也不能直接套這個分解。ESMM 是理解條件機率與樣本選擇問題的參考。

The factorization is valid only when purchase explicitly means a conversion after a click and pCVR = P(purchase | click, exposure, x). Then P(click | exposure, x) × pCVR represents that path's purchase rate. If a head directly predicts P(purchase | exposure, x), multiplying by pCTR discounts it twice. View-through purchases also require different semantics. ESMM is a useful reference for conditional probabilities and sample selection.

Example policy objective, not an Amazon formula:
U(u,i) = P(buy | exposure,x) * [1 - P(return | buy,x)] - lambda * fatigue(u,i)
Use compatible cohorts/windows; normalize penalties before combining scales.
The policy score U is not itself a probability.

If return labels exist only for fulfilled purchases, do not treat that head as the return rate for all purchases without aligning cohorts or modeling fulfillment/cancellation separately. Monetary utility may use expected net value, but monitor bias toward expensive items and long-term experience. Calibration does not replace online utility validation.

為什麼更複雜的序列模型可能輸給 GBDT？Why might a complex sequence model lose to GBDT?

There may be too few mature labels, short sequences, sufficient structured features, or a mismatch between training negatives and serving candidates. I would compare on the same candidate pool and chronological split, with reasonable tuning and comparable cost, then break down performance by session length and cold start. Complexity is not an outcome; quality, latency, and cost are.

Only under explicit assumptions. The logging policy needs support for actions being evaluated, with usable and correctly logged propensities; joint slate probabilities are especially difficult. Inspect extreme weights and effective sample size, and disclose the bias–variance tradeoff if clipping. Weighting cannot invent evidence for never-exposed new items or unsupported policy regions.

Offline NDCG 上升、線上購買下降，怎麼排查？Offline NDCG rises but online purchases fall. What next?

先確認實驗有效與 serving 版本／特徵一致，再查離線洩漏、抽樣候選過於容易、負例與線上不同、庫存過濾及 latency 變化。接著按新品、locale、session 長度拆解，檢查是不是點擊誘人但購買價值差，或只是把其他 surface 的訂單搬過來。不要只重新調一個 loss 權重就結案。

First verify experiment validity and serving version/feature consistency. Then check leakage, easy sampled candidates, training–serving negative mismatch, inventory filtering, and latency. Slice by new items, locale, and session length. Determine whether the model attracts clicks without purchase value or moves orders from another surface. Do not stop after adjusting one loss weight.

Duration depends on MDE, variance, and label maturity. I would measure mature valid orders and site-wide value to detect mere redistribution across surfaces.

40–45 分鐘：失敗案例與取捨40–45 minutes: failures and tradeoffs

若買完相機仍看到相機，我先查購買訊號延遲與 mission state，再調耐用品抑制和相容配件。若新品沒曝光，檢查 OOV、內容表示、索引更新與合格探索。若庫存大量淘汰，改善 early filter 與有界補取。若離線好但線上差，先查實驗與 train–serve skew，再查目標與偏差。我會保留強 baseline，只有收益足以支付延遲與維運成本才增加複雜度。

If cameras persist after purchase, I would inspect purchase-signal lag and mission state before changing durable-item suppression and compatible accessories. For unseen new products, check OOV handling, content representations, index freshness, and qualified exploration. For high stock rejection, improve early filtering and bounded refill. If offline gains fail online, first check experiments and train–serve skew, then objectives and bias. I would retain strong baselines and add complexity only when gains justify latency and operational cost.
