01 / The conversation, before the model

{{每題，用同一條主線回答。 || One consistent story for every case.}}

先確認「替誰做什麼決定、怎麼知道成功」，再選模型。每一步都說出一個具體選擇、支持它的理由，以及什麼證據會讓你改變選擇。

First establish whose decision the system improves and how success will be measured. At each stage, state a concrete choice, its rationale, and the evidence that would make you revisit it.

01 · 00–05 MIN

{{釐清產品目標 || Clarify product goals}}

入口、使用者、決策、主指標、護欄、QPS 與延遲。

Surface, users, decision, primary metric, guardrails, QPS, latency.

02 · 05–12 MIN

{{資料與標籤 || Data & labels}}

一筆樣本是什麼？標籤何時成熟？如何避免洩漏？

What is an example? When is its label mature? Where could leakage occur?

03 · 12–22 MIN

{{Baseline／模型 || Baseline & models}}

先給 baseline，再說召回、排序、loss 與升級理由。

Baseline first, then retrieval, ranking, loss, and reasons to add complexity.

04 · 22–32 MIN

{{訓練與部署 || Training & deployment}}

資料流、版本、更新頻率、延遲預算、fallback、rollback。

Pipelines, versions, freshness, latency budgets, fallbacks, rollback.

05 · 32–40 MIN

{{離線評估／線上實驗 || Offline / online evaluation}}

時間切分、切片、主指標、分流、SRM、樣本量與成熟窗口。

Temporal splits, slices, metrics, assignment, SRM, power, label maturity.

06 · 40–45 MIN

{{失敗案例與取捨 || Failures & tradeoffs}}

給具體故障：怎麼發現、如何降級、何時值得增加複雜度。

Give a concrete failure, detection signal, degraded path, and complexity tradeoff.

白板最少畫兩條路徑：線上 request → features/retrieval → scoring → constraints → response；離線 events → labels → training → validation → versioned deployment。用 outcome logs 把兩者接起來。

Draw two paths: online request → features/retrieval → scoring → constraints → response; offline events → labels → training → validation → versioned deployment. Connect them through outcome logs.

{{可以直接練的轉場句 || Ready-to-rehearse transitions}}
  1. 「在選模型前，我想先確認我們要改善哪個決策，以及不能犧牲的護欄。」“Before choosing a model, I would clarify the decision we are improving and the guardrails we cannot compromise.”
  2. 「我會把一筆訓練樣本定義為……，並只使用決策當下已知的特徵。」“I would define one training example as … and use only features available at decision time.”
  3. 「先用這個 baseline 驗證資料；只有錯誤分析顯示需要時，才增加模型複雜度。」“This baseline validates the data pipeline. I would add complexity only when error analysis identifies a limitation it can address.”
  4. 「現在我把它放到線上：這是 critical path、時間預算，以及依賴失效時的回退方式。」“For serving, this is the critical path, its latency budget, and the fallback when a dependency fails.”
  5. 「離線改善是篩選條件；是否真的改善產品，要靠可信的線上實驗。」“Offline improvement is a screening criterion; a trustworthy online experiment establishes product impact.”
  6. 「如果這個指標改善、但護欄退化，我會先檢查……，而不是直接擴大上線。」“If the primary metric improves but a guardrail regresses, I would investigate … before expanding the rollout.”

03 / Explain the metric, not just its name

{{評估速查：每個指標回答什麼？ || What does each metric actually tell you?}}

{{指標 || Metric}}{{定義與用途 || Meaning & use}}{{常見盲點 || Pitfall}}
Recall@K{{Top-K 找回的相關項目數 / 全部已知相關項目數。看候選階段有沒有漏掉好結果。 || Relevant items in the top K divided by all known relevant items. Diagnoses candidate omissions.}}{{真實相關集合常不完整，需說明標籤來源；不是 ANN 與 exact kNN 的一致率。 || Relevance labels may be incomplete. Specify their source; this is not ANN agreement with exact kNN.}}
NDCG@K{{用位置折扣計算排序效用，再除以理想排序。常用 DCG = Σ(2^rel−1)/log₂(rank+1)。 || Discounts relevance by rank, then normalizes by ideal ranking. A common DCG is Σ(2^rel−1)/log₂(rank+1).}}{{需定義 relevance 等級與零 IDCG 的處理；分數好不保證增量產品價值。 || Define relevance grades and zero-IDCG handling. Gains do not establish incremental product value.}}
ROC-AUC / PR-AUC{{ROC-AUC 看正例是否排在負例前；PR-AUC 聚焦 precision/recall。 || ROC-AUC measures positive-versus-negative ordering; PR-AUC summarizes precision/recall.}}{{不衡量機率校準；PR-AUC 依正例率而變，不宜跨不同抽樣分布直接比較。 || Neither measures calibration. PR-AUC depends on prevalence and is not directly comparable across different sampling distributions.}}
Log loss / Brier{{Binary log loss = −mean[y log p+(1−y) log(1−p)]；Brier = mean[(p−y)²]。衡量機率預測。 || Binary log loss = −mean[y log p+(1−y) log(1−p)]; Brier = mean[(p−y)²]. Both score probability predictions.}}{{混合了校準與區分能力；要搭配 reliability diagram、切片和自然分布評估。 || They combine calibration and discrimination effects. Add reliability diagrams, slices, and natural-distribution evaluation.}}
Groundedness / citations{{檢查可驗證 claim 是否有提供的證據支持；引用 precision 看所引來源是否支持 claim。 || Check whether verifiable claims are supported by supplied evidence; citation precision checks support from the cited source.}}{{有引用不等於來源正確、時效適用或回答完整；正確性、coverage、權限需分開測。 || Citations do not establish source truth, freshness, or completeness. Evaluate correctness, coverage, and access separately.}}
MAE / quantile coverage{{MAE 看平均絕對誤差；預測 p90 應約有 90% 結果低於它，需按切片驗證。 || MAE measures average absolute error; about 90% of outcomes should fall below a calibrated p90 prediction, including within slices.}}{{平均值可掩蓋極端低估；MAPE 對接近零的標籤不穩。 || Means can hide severe underestimation; MAPE is unstable near zero.}}
p95 / p99 / cost{{端到端量測尾延遲，同時報吞吐、錯誤率與每成功任務成本。 || Measure end-to-end tail latency together with throughput, error rate, and cost per successful task.}}{{階段 p95 不能直接相加；平均 latency 與 cache-hit-only 數字會掩蓋長尾。 || Stage p95 values are not additive. Mean latency and cache-hit-only measurements hide tails.}}
A/B · SRM · MDE{{先定分流單位、主指標、最小可偵測效果與樣本量；SRM 檢查實際分組比例是否異常。 || Predefine randomization unit, primary metric, minimum detectable effect, and sample size. SRM checks anomalous allocation ratios.}}{{先修資料問題；重複偷看、組間干擾、樣本不成熟都可能讓結論失真。 || Resolve data issues first. Peeking, interference, and immature outcomes can invalidate conclusions.}}

實驗回答要落地：假設主指標為 orders/user，就明確說 user-level 穩定分流、事前以使用者層級變異估 power、定義 MDE 和觀察窗。先查 SRM 與日誌品質，再看效果量和信賴區間；不因某天 p<0.05 就提早結束。若用 sequential testing，需事先定義有效的序列檢定。廣告預算、社交關係或市場供需可能需要不同分流設計。

Make the experiment concrete: for orders/user, use stable user assignment, power calculations based on user-level variance, a predefined MDE, and a fixed observation window. Check SRM/logging before effect sizes and confidence intervals. Do not stop merely because one day's p-value drops below 0.05. Sequential testing needs a valid prespecified procedure. Shared ad budgets, social ties, and marketplaces may require different assignment designs.

04 / Rehearse, diagnose, repeat

{{口說完，檢查六件事。 || Six checks after your rehearsal.}}

01 · {{目標有決策單位 || A concrete decision unit}}

能說清楚誰、在哪個入口、選擇什麼，以及一個主指標和至少兩個護欄。

Name the user, surface, decision, one primary metric, and at least two guardrails.

02 · {{標籤有時間語意 || Labels have time semantics}}

能說出一筆樣本、正負例、成熟窗口、抽樣偏差與一個洩漏風險。

Define one example, positives/negatives, maturity, sampling bias, and one leakage risk.

03 · {{模型選擇有理由 || Model choices have reasons}}

先給 baseline，解釋 loss 與輸出，說清楚何時值得加深模型。

Give a baseline, explain the loss/output, and specify when complexity becomes worthwhile.

04 · {{線上路徑可運作 || Serving is operational}}

畫出資料流、版本和新鮮度；有容量估算、timeout fallback 與 rollback。

Draw data flow, versions, and freshness. Include capacity, timeout fallback, and rollback.

05 · {{評估能辨識問題 || Evaluation isolates failures}}

分開元件與端到端指標，說明切分、切片、分流、護欄與實驗可信度。

Separate component and whole-system metrics; explain splits, slices, assignment, and experiment validity.

06 · {{取捨可被檢驗 || Tradeoffs are testable}}

至少說一個 failure → detection → mitigation，並用品質／延遲／成本決定下一步。

State a failure → detection → mitigation chain, and choose the next step using quality, latency, and cost.

建議練習節奏：先用三次各 45 分鐘練 Ads、Products、RAG。第四次補 Feed＋Visual Search 的差異；第五次做購物助理整合。之後依弱點選 Training／Cost／Moderation，其他題目用六步流程練遷移。每次先閉卷，記下卡住的一步，再讀答案重講。

Suggested sequence: use three 45-minute sessions for Ads, Products, and RAG. Next, compare Feed and Visual Search, then tackle the shopping assistant. Add Training, Cost, or Moderation where you need depth; use the remaining cases to practice transfer. Rehearse without answers, identify the stage where you stalled, then read and explain it again.
